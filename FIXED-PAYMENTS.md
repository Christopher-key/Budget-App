# Savings & Payoffs: fixed monthly payments

In the website's **Savings & Payoffs** tab, use **+ Fixed payment** for a loan,
car payment, lease, insurance policy or another fixed monthly obligation. Savings
buckets and the monthly dividend calculator remain available in the same tab.

Enter a name, monthly amount, expense category, due day and starting month.
The final month is optional and inclusive. **Apply to Budgeted expenses and Home**
is off for new entries; check it when you want the payment included.

An applied payment appears once in the Budgeted pay period containing its due
day, and in a monthly preview on Home. A due day beyond month end uses the last
day, such as February 28/29 for a payment due on day 31. Changing pay schedules
moves the fixed line to the correct period while preserving monthly totals.
Unapplied entries stay in the manager and contribute nothing to the budget.

Fixed amounts are additional to manually entered category amounts. If Car already
contains your loan payment, adjust that manual amount before applying the same
payment here. Keep the manual field for other expenses in that category.

Edits and unapplying update this month and saved future plans; earlier monthly
snapshots retain their names and amounts. To retain this month's payment but stop
later months, set the final month to this month. Future forecasts include eligible
fixed payments without duplicating them when a current plan is copied. Home,
monthly summaries, category warnings, charts and Excel totals use the same
Budgeted expense entries. No Realistic spending is generated by a planned payment.

## Optional payoff and interest tracking

Enable **Track an optional payoff balance** for an obligation with a remaining
balance. Enter its current statement balance and an APR or APY from 0% to 100%,
with up to four decimal places. Use 0 for an interest-free payoff.

The live estimate shows monthly payments remaining, a smaller final payment when
appropriate, and remaining interest. APR is divided into twelve monthly rates;
APY uses its equivalent monthly compounded rate. Interest is rounded to cents
before each payment, then principal is reduced. An insufficient/interest-only
payment has no finite payoff estimate. Calculations beyond 1,200 monthly payments
are identified as beyond the estimate limit rather than given a guessed count.

**Record payment** updates the payoff tracker and its self-reported history using
the interest you confirm in the dialog. It caps a final payment at the balance
plus that interest. **Report spending separately in Transactions** to update Realistic
expenses. The tracker does not connect to a lender or mark a payment made merely
because a due date passed. Daily interest, fees and statement adjustments can
change the actual payoff; use Edit to reconcile the balance against a statement.

The recording dialog's **Interest included** amount starts with one month's
estimate. Replace it with your statement interest; enter 0 for a principal-only
extra payment. This prevents an extra payment from unintentionally adding another
full month of interest. The balance reduction and capped final payment use the
interest you confirm in that dialog.

A completed payoff retains this month's Budgeted amount and stops later plans.
Completing a payoff before its starting month leaves it unapplied. Recording a
tracker payment does not reduce savings or create an income/expense transaction.
Excel reports include planned fixed costs in their category/pay-period/monthly
totals; the new payoff-tracker history is not a separate workbook sheet.

## Privacy and compatibility

Definitions, statement balances, rates, history and linked plan snapshots stay
inside the existing device-encrypted ledger. The API receives the same opaque
ciphertext envelope; this website release needs no new backend endpoint or bank
connection. Limits are 200 definitions, 1,000 tracker records across them, and the
existing one-MiB ledger limit. Forgotten-password and account-erasure rules remain
the same.

Existing ledgers without fixed payments retain their previous shape. Once this
optional feature is used, older browser code rejects unsupported fields instead
of silently dropping them. Stale writes are rejected by the existing revision
check. Refresh older browser tabs after the update to load the new interface.
The paused native cloud integration will need this feature's model/UI support
before it can claim web parity; it is not changed by this release.
